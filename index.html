Tutor · Pharmacology
Why does an oral dose reach the blood at a lower level than an IV one?
Because of the first-pass effect: a drug absorbed from the gut goes to the liver before it reaches the circulation, and the liver metabolises part of it 1. What gets through is the bioavailability, 100% for IV and less for oral 2.

What happens to the cell in caseous necrosis?

Privacy

Your library lives on your computer.

Nota doesn't have accounts or a cloud copy of your files. Reading, search and memory all run locally.

  • Your files are read and indexed on your computer. They are never uploaded.
  • To answer a question, only the few passages it needs go to the AI provider you chose, with your own key.
  • Your key is stored only on your computer, and only Nota itself can change it.
  • Nota counts installs anonymously: once a day, that it was opened, its version and a random install number. Never your name, files or questions, and one switch turns it off.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Nota really free?

Yes. Nota is free, and the AI providers it uses, Google Gemini, Groq and Cerebras, all have free tiers that cover normal studying. You make a key with your own account, so there's no bill from us.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is it safe?

Windows shows this for new apps that haven't built up a download reputation yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. The installer only puts Nota in your user folder and doesn't need administrator rights.

What files can I add?

PDFs (slides exported as PDF work well), Word documents (.docx), EPUB books, Markdown and plain text, web pages by link, and notes you paste in. Scanned PDFs with no text layer need the free Tesseract OCR installed.

Does it work offline?

Your library, reading, search and calendar all work offline. Answers, quizzes and skims need the internet, because they use the AI provider.

Mac or Linux?

Nota is Windows-only for now.
